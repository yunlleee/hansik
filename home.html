HANKSIK FINE DINING

Hanksik is a modern fine-dining experience located in the heart of the city. Whether you're enjoying the company of friends in our dining room or getting a bird’s-eye view of the action around the chef’s counter, guests will experience warm and welcoming hospitality that’s at the heart of everything we do.
